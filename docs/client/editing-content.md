# How to edit content on the Ilot website

You edit all categories, sub-categories, and services through the Studio at:

**https://<your-domain>/studio**

Log in with the email address you were invited with. If you lose access, ask the developer to re-invite you.

## What you can edit

- **Categories** (Visa & Immigration, Legal, etc.) — name, tagline, icon, accent colour, cover image, sort order
- **Sub-Categories** (Investor KITAS, Visit Visas, etc.) — name, parent category, sort order
- **Services** (the individual offerings) — name, description, target client, key deliverables, timeline, real-time work, WhatsApp message, SEO fields

## Editing workflow

1. Find the document in the left sidebar: **Categories → click a category → Sub-Categories → click a sub-category → Services**
2. Click the document you want to edit
3. Make your changes — every text field has an **English** tab and a **Bahasa Indonesia** tab. If Bahasa is left empty, the site automatically shows the English version
4. Click **Publish** in the top-right corner
5. Changes appear on the live site within approximately **1 minute**

## Important rules

- **Do not change a slug** on any existing category, sub-category, or service. The slug is part of its URL — changing it breaks links and SEO. If a slug needs to change, contact the developer.
- **Do not delete a category or sub-category that has services attached.** Move or reassign the services first, then delete the parent.
- **Drafts are auto-saved** — you can close the browser and come back later without losing work. Click Publish only when you are ready for changes to go live.

## Adding new content

- **New sub-category:** Click the + button in the Sub-Categories list under the relevant category
- **New service:** Click the + button in the Services list under the relevant sub-category
- Fill in the English fields at minimum, then Publish
