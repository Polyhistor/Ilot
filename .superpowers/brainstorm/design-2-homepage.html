Hero Scroll Animation — 3 Scenes
SCENE 1 — On Load
Headline centered, 7 cards sit in a horizontal strip at the bottom edge.
SCENE 2 — Mid-Scroll
Text fades & slides up. Cards fly outward along curved paths, rotating as they travel.
SCENE 3 — Scroll Complete
Cards locked in a circle, each tilted outward like clock numbers. Center reveals new headline.

Rotation in circle: each card tilted to face outward from center
Implementation Approach
• Framer Motion useScroll + useTransform — scroll progress (0→1) drives all values
• Sticky container ~300vh — keeps canvas fixed while user scrolls 3 screen-heights
• Each card has its own x, y, rotate spring targets derived from scroll progress
• Text opacity: 1→0 in first 30% of scroll range
• Cards travel 0→finalCirclePosition in 30–100% of scroll range
• Center "secondary headline" fades in at 90–100% of scroll range
